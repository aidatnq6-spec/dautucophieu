Unknown rights, timestamps or freshness do not pass. A fetched timestamp is not a publication timestamp. Financial-period expectations must come from a dated reporting calendar or filing evidence; do not invent a universal filing deadline. Synthetic fixtures use a weekday schedule for exercising numeric comparison, not the real HOSE holiday calendar. They can never establish live coverage or entitlement. The aggregate audit reports distinct eligible issuers and keeps the 50-live-issuer gate closed until a trusted source workflow exists. This aggregate is separate from approving a research universe.
